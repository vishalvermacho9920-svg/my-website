The important reality check

AI makes the clips. The studio makes the film.

No model on earth generates a coherent 40-minute video in one inference — and any tool that claims to is hiding the seams. AI Video Studio is explicit about the boundary: every short clip is generated on its own, then a deterministic editing pipeline stitches them into a single, reliable MP4.

What the AI generates

Short, high-quality video clips — one model call per shot. This is where diffusion models actually shine.

  • 4–12 second clips per generation call
  • Full per-scene control: camera, shot, lighting, mood
  • Character seeds locked for cross-scene consistency
  • Retry or regenerate any single shot without touching the rest

What the studio assembles

Deterministic, offline, fully controllable editing — the part that makes a long video actually watchable.

  • Automatic timeline arrangement of every clip
  • TTS narration, subtitle timing, music mixing
  • FFmpeg encode: transitions, loudness normalisation, MP4
  • Re-render only what changed — never the whole project

In this interactive demo, clip generation, voiceover and rendering are simulated locally in your browser so you can explore the complete workflow instantly. In a self-hosted deployment those same buttons call your local ComfyUI / FFmpeg / TTS workers — the interface, queue, timeline and project format stay identical.

Questions, answered

Everything beginners ask first

Yes — but not in one model call, and we are upfront about that. Your script is divided into scenes, each scene into short clips, and every clip is generated individually. The timeline then stitches them into one continuous video up to 40 minutes. This is the only reliable way to produce long-form AI video today.

Not necessarily. Clip generation is the only GPU-heavy stage and can run on a modest consumer GPU, on a rented worker, or against a hosted model of your choice. Scripting, timeline editing, voiceover, subtitles and rendering all run comfortably on an ordinary laptop.

Each character in the library carries a locked description string, appearance tags and a seed. Scenes that reference the same character inherit those values, along with palette and lighting memory from the previous scene, so connected shots stay visually coherent instead of drifting.

Regenerate just that clip. Because clips are generated individually and arranged on a timeline, you can retry any shot, adjust its prompt, camera move or duration, and re-render — without touching the rest of the project.

The bundled music library is royalty-safe and cleared for commercial use. Voices are generated from open-source TTS models that you run yourself. If you upload your own audio, the studio reminds you to confirm your licence before rendering.

Projects are stored locally in your browser and export to a portable JSON file. API keys are encrypted in a local vault, resolved only by the worker process, and never embedded in the frontend bundle or sent to analytics.
